Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Americano Padel (the website at americano-padel.space.fast and the Android app of the same name) helps you run padel tournaments. This policy explains what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Tournament data you enter: player names (or nicknames), scores, and tournament settings. This is stored on our server so you can share a live link, and is automatically deleted 7 days after the last update.
  • On your device: a random edit token and admin code for tournaments you create or administer, saved in your browser/app local storage so you can keep editing. Clearing app data removes them.
  • Basic traffic counts: our host (Spacefast) counts requests, page views and unique visitors at its edge. We don't add analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

What we don't collect

No accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, advertising IDs or payment information. We don't show ads and don't sell or share data with third parties. Please don't enter real names of people who haven't agreed to it; nicknames work fine.

Sharing

Anyone with a tournament link can view that tournament's player names and scores. Anyone with its admin code can edit scores. Share links only with people you trust.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deletion and contact

Tournament data expires automatically after 7 days. To request earlier deletion, open an issue at github.com/joashrajin/Americano-Padel.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
